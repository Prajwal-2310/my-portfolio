About Me

My Journey

I have completed my Bachelor's degree in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering and am currently pursuing a Post Graduate Diploma in Advanced Computing (PG-DAC) at CDAC Kharghar.

During engineering, I worked on projects such as an accident detection system and an AI-based robotic car, gaining exposure to both hardware and software. These experiences developed my interest in programming and problem-solving and motivated me to pursue software development.

At CDAC, I am strengthening my skills in C++, Java, Database Management Systems (DBMS), Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA), and web technologies. I enjoy solving programming problems and understanding the logic behind algorithms.

Apart from academics, I served as a Co-Sports Secretary at the college level, which helped me develop teamwork and coordination skills. I also secured third rank in engineering.

My goal is to begin my career as a software developer, continuously improve my technical skills, and contribute to building effective software solutions.
